## Problem Statement

During 2018, the smartphone market was experiencing slowing growth and
changes in consumer replacement behavior. Apple also experienced significant
pressure during this period and also raising the question of whether movements in
the focal company were reflected in companies connected to its supply chain.

This project investigates the potential ripple effects associated with
Apple's 2018 market decline by examining Apple as the focal company,
selected upstream suppliers, and a broader technology-sector benchmark XLK.

## Assets

- AAPL — Focal Company
- QCOM — Upstream Supplier
- GLW — Upstream Supplier
- XLK — Technology Sector Benchmark

## Research Questions

- How strongly did movements in AAPL correspond with movements in its
  selected upstream suppliers?
- Did significant movements in the upstream companies occur before or
  after the major movement in AAPL?
- How large was the relative impact on AAPL, QCOM, and GLW?
- When did the decline begin to recover?
- How did market conditions before, during, and after the crisis differ?
- To what extent were these movements specific to the selected companies
  versus broader technology-sector movements?
